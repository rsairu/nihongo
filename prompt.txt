Task:
Provide Japanese readings (よみがな) for words that are NOT present in known_words.json.

Processing Steps:
1. 写真の本文を 全文OCR（縦書き対応）
   - Perform full OCR on photo text with vertical writing support
2. 単語単位に分解
   - Break down the text into word units

Normalization:
Apply all normalization steps before checking known_words.json.

Normalization rules:
- Ignore words written in katakana.
- Reduce words to their dictionary (base) form.
  - Ignore conjugations, declensions, polite forms, and inflected variants.
  - For サ変 verbs, normalize to the noun form.
- Treat kanji and kana equivalents as the same word.
  - If a word exists in known_words.json in kanji form, ignore its kana-only form.
    Example:
    - known_words.json contains: 出来る
    - Ignore: できる
- Ignore numeric prefixes.
  - Strip leading numbers written in kanji or digits.
  - Re-evaluate the remaining word after stripping.
    Examples:
    - 七年間 → 年間
    - 3回目 → 回目
    - 数ヶ月 → ヶ月
- Ignore ONLY non-lexical suffixes, e.g.; 中 / 前 / 後 / 済み / 以上 / 以下 / 未満
  - These suffixes must NOT form an independent noun.
  - Do NOT strip if the remaining word would lose core meaning.

Evaluation:
- After normalization, check against known_words.json.
- If the normalized form exists, ignore it.
- Otherwise, ALWAYS include the word, even if abstract or explanatory.

Output:
- For each valid word, output:
  - the word
  - its standard Japanese reading in hiragana
- Do not explain ignored words.
- Do not include extra commentary.

Flashcard Selection:
- After processing all words, if there are multiple unknown words, select (up to) the top 3 words based on:
  - Word frequency (頻度) in Japanese language usage
  - Foundationalness to the language (core vocabulary, essential for communication)
- Priority order:
  1. High-frequency words that appear commonly in everyday Japanese
  2. Foundational words that are essential building blocks for language learning
  3. Words that combine both frequency and foundational importance

Flashcard Output:
- For each of the top 3 selected words, provide:
  1. Word (in original kanji/kana form)
  2. Reading (よみがな in hiragana)
  3. Simple definition (concise, suitable for flashcards - 1-2 sentences max)
  4. Example sentences (2-3 sentences showing natural usage)
     - Use varied contexts to demonstrate different uses of the word
- Format the flashcard output clearly, separating each word with a blank line
