Task:
Provide Japanese readings (よみがな) for words that are NOT present in known_words.json.

Normalization:
Apply all normalization steps before checking known_words.json.

Normalization rules:
- Ignore words written in katakana.
- Reduce words to their dictionary (base) form.
  - Ignore conjugations, declensions, polite forms, and inflected variants.
- Treat kanji and kana equivalents as the same word.
  - If a word exists in known_words.json in kanji form, ignore its kana-only form.
    Example:
    - known_words.json contains: 出来る
    - Ignore: できる
- Ignore numeric prefixes.
  - Strip leading numbers written in kanji or digits.
  - Re-evaluate the remaining word after stripping.
    Examples:
    - 七年間 → 年間
    - 3回目 → 回目
    - 数ヶ月 → ヶ月
- Ignore semantic suffixes.
  - Strip common non-lexical suffixes indicating state, time, range, or completion.
  - Re-evaluate the remaining word after stripping.
    Examples:
    - 使用中 → 使用
    - 予定前 → 予定
    - 作業済み → 作業
    - 以上 / 以下 / 未満 → remove suffix only

Evaluation:
- After normalization, check the resulting word against known_words.json.
- If the normalized word exists in known_words.json, ignore the original word.
- Only process words that remain unknown after normalization.

Output:
- For each valid word, output:
  - the word
  - its standard Japanese reading in hiragana
- Do not explain ignored words.
- Do not include extra commentary.
